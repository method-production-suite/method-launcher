# Method Launcher 0.1.0

Method Launcher's first release: one small app that installs the Method apps, checks each download before anything runs, and keeps them all up to date. Open it, pick the apps you want, or press Demo the suite to see them working together on Field Station.

## Every app in one place

- **See where each app stands.** Each app's card shows whether it is installed, which
  version, and whether a newer one is out, with the day it was released and a line on
  what's new.
- **Open, or show it in the Finder.** One click opens an app; installed apps are found
  wherever they are in your Applications folders, even under an earlier name.
- **Your recent files, across the suite.** The files you opened lately in any Method app,
  newest first, each opening in the app it came from.
- **Demo the suite.** One button opens every installed app on the invented Field Station
  production: the script, the PDF, the schedule and the budget.

## Install and update, checked first

- **Install with one click.** On a Mac, the launcher downloads the app from its own
  release, then checks that it is signed by the suite, notarised by Apple and built for
  Apple silicon before anything is copied. If a check fails, nothing is installed.
- **Update one app, or all of them.** An update goes through the same checks. If the app is
  open, the launcher asks first, then asks the app to quit, so it can ask you about anything
  unsaved.
- **Checks you can turn off.** The launcher looks for new versions when it opens; turn
  "Check for updates automatically" off and it looks only when you press Refresh.

## The suite's look

- The Method Production Suite's mark and colours, in light and dark, and your system's own
  type.

## Not there yet

- Installing and updating from the launcher works on macOS. On Windows and Linux the
  launcher shows your apps and their versions, and links each app's download page.
- The launcher doesn't update itself yet. When a new version is out, it says so at the top
  of its window, with a link to the download.
- A Settings window and the Help menu come next.
